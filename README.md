# 🛡️ HSSE Reporting Platform

## Overview

The **HSSE Reporting Platform** is an incident reporting and monitoring system developed for a hackathon. The platform was designed to improve **Health, Safety, Security, and Environment (HSSE)** awareness by enabling the public to report incidents directly to relevant businesses and government agencies, while also allowing organizations to monitor emerging HSSE-related issues from online sources.

The system combines user-generated incident reporting, geolocation mapping, and an AI-powered web scraping component to provide organizations with timely, actionable information.

---

## Purpose

The primary goal of the platform is to:

-   Simplify the process of reporting HSSE-related incidents
-   Improve incident visibility and response for organizations
-   Centralize both direct reports and externally sourced HSSE intelligence

---

## Key Features

### Public Incident Reporting

-   Users can submit HSSE incident reports to specific **organization or agency accounts**
-   Reports include:
    -   Incident description
    -   Location data selected via an **interactive map**
    -   Additional contextual details relevant to the incident

---

### Map-Based Location Reporting

-   Users can pinpoint incident locations by clicking on a map
-   Enhances accuracy of reports and improves situational awareness
-   Supports faster assessment and response by receiving organizations

---

### Organization & Agency Accounts

-   Dedicated accounts for businesses and agencies
-   Organizations can:
    -   View all incident reports submitted to them
    -   Access detailed incident information, including location and descriptions
    -   Monitor trends related to their operations or areas of responsibility

---

### AI-Powered Web Scraping & Monitoring

-   Integrated AI web scraper monitors **news sites and other platforms**
-   Automatically checks for:
    -   HSSE-related incidents
    -   News, alerts, and discussions relevant to a specific organization
-   The application automatically scans selected news sources **every 30 hours**
-   When relevant information is detected:
    -   An **email alert** is sent to the admin
    -   An **in-app notification** is generated for follow-up action

This ensures organizations stay informed about potential HSSE risks even when incidents are not directly reported through the platform.
